Hey {{first_name}},
We’re getting closer to Fix Your Store Before Black Friday on 5 October, and there’s something I want you to know before we start.
My approach to getting a Shopify store Black Friday ready is probably a little different from most of the advice you’ll see flying around over the next few months.
You don’t need a list of 57 things you should probably do — add a countdown timer, install another app, change the announcement bar, create a popup, add reviews, build a bundle, change your homepage — and suddenly you’re frantically polishing whatever happens to catch your eye.
That’s not the approach we’re taking. Instead, we’ll work through your store in a specific order:
Simplify → Beautify → Amplify
First, make it clear. Then, make it feel trustworthy and polished. Then start adding the things designed to increase conversions.
Because urgency on top of a confusing store doesn’t fix the problem. More traffic to a product page people don’t trust doesn’t fix the problem either. And a beautiful website that makes it hard to buy still isn’t doing its job.
During the challenge, I want to help you step back from all the Black Friday noise and work out what’s genuinely worth fixing in your store.
Something you can do before we start
Go to your store on your phone and pretend you’ve never seen it before. Give yourself 10 seconds. Can you immediately tell:
• What do you sell?
• Why should I care?
• What should I click next?
Don’t fix anything yet. Just notice. We’ll get into the rest together from 5–9 October.
