Day 2 · Simplify
Reading The Numbers
The numbers tell you where to look,
not why.
If you see…Look at…
Visits, but few add to cart Can they find and understand the product? Price, stock, traffic source.
Adds to cart, but don’t continue The cart: can they check their items and see the next step?
Reach checkout, but don’t finish Delivery options, total cost, payment steps.
Leave with one clue and one place to look.

Clearer Copy
Fact → benefit → details.

Breathable linen bedding for everyday comfort. Includes one duvet cover and two pillowcases. Choose your bed size below.

Keep the benefit believable. Check every claim against what you sell.
