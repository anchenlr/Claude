Add-Ons & Upgrades
Easy to say yes.
Just as easy to say no.
Cross-Sell
Something that works with their choice (the fitted sheet).
Upsell
A different option with a difference that matters.
“More premium” says nothing. Size, material and what’s included say a lot.
Placement: Product page — they’re still deciding · Cart — quick to understand, easy to decline, checkout still obvious

Delivery Thresholds
Free delivery: check the real rule.
Before you pick a threshold: Usual order values · What people could realistically add · Shipping cost by region
Wording must match: Regions · Service · Minimum spend · Exclusions
Other discounts can change whether a cart qualifies. Test the real combination with a real address.

Keep It Honest
Urgency has to be real.
Real Reasons
  • ✓  A real end date
  • ✓  A limited batch
  • ✓  A genuine delivery cut-off
Not Okay
  • ✗  Timers that restart for every visitor
  • ✗  Stock levels that don’t match reality
Put the date and time zone in your terms. No deadline? A clear, useful offer still works.
