Day 3 · Beautify
Clearer Layout
Same facts, easier to find.
Before
“The fabric is 100% linen. The set includes one duvet cover and two pillowcases. Choose your size before adding the set to your cart.”
Where would you look to check what’s included?
After
Fabric: 100% linen
In the set: One duvet cover and two pillowcases
Choose your size: Choose your size before adding the set to your cart.
No badge, no photo, no app. Just existing info, easier to use.
Illustrative product details.
