How next week will work

Our goal is that you don't have to worry about a thing, other than showing up for anything live and popping into the Facebook group when you can. Here's how it works:

Your release time. Your presentation goes live at your scheduled time, which you can see in your hub, in your own time zone.
Live Q&A on the same page. If you're doing a live Q&A, it starts 45 minutes after your presentation releases, right on your presentation page. Attendees watch your session and stay on that page for the Q&A, so there's only one place to go.
Attendee Hub. Attendees build their own personal summit plan and add sessions to their calendar, so more of them turn up for yours.
48 hours to watch. Your presentation stays free to watch for 48 hours. Any time in that window, feel free to jump into the Facebook group to answer questions, go live or just hang out with attendees.

Joined us before? The big changes this year are that each presentation has its own release time (rather than everyone at 12 PM UK), and the live Q&A happens right on your presentation page.
