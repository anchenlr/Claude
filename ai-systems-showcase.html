The Session

Beyond the Download: Using AI to Create a Premium Post-Purchase Experience

A purchase confirmation followed by a plain download link technically does the job. But it misses the opportunity to guide the customer, reinforce the value of what they bought, and make a small digital product feel considered from beginning to end. In this session, I walked through the system I built to make that handoff better — without manually recreating and sending the same resources after every sale.

1

A customer purchases

Shopify captures the order and product information.

2

Make starts the workflow

The automation receives the customer and purchase details.

3

Airtable supplies the content

Product-specific code, instructions, links and resources are pulled from the database.

4

AI assembles the resource

The system turns the relevant information into a useful, branded HTML playbook.

5

The customer receives a premium handoff

They receive more than a file: they receive context, guidance and a clear next step.
