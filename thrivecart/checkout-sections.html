Introducing

Shopify SEO, made simple

Shopify SEO, Simplified is a step-by-step course for anyone who wants to understand and apply Shopify SEO with confidence — including DIY Shopify store owners, designers, freelancers, and service providers.

It walks you through a clear, practical system for optimising Shopify stores without overcomplicating the process.

Inside, you'll learn how to:

  • identify the pages that actually drive revenue
  • optimise product and collection pages with confidence
  • simplify keyword strategy so it works for real stores
  • structure internal links to support conversions
  • maintain SEO in a way that compounds over time

Whether you're optimising your own store or delivering Shopify SEO as a service, this training gives you a clear framework you can apply immediately.
