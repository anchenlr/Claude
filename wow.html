What's Inside the Checklist

In my session, "Design For Conversion," I shared the 20-point checklist I use on every ecommerce project, at discovery to spot what's costing a store sales, during wireframes and build as a design checklist, and at handover so the store keeps improving after launch.

Simplify: Clarity

Seven checks for value proposition, navigation, product titles and search, so visitors immediately understand what you sell.

Beautify: Confidence

Six checks for photography, page structure, reviews and trust, so visitors believe what you're telling them.

Amplify: Momentum

Five checks for add to cart, checkout and email capture, so visitors actually complete the purchase.

Across the Journey

Two checks for mobile experience and site speed that affect every single page.
