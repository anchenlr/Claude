Presentation guidance

This year's theme: What's Working Now

The web industry is changing quickly, so we want this summit to feel current, practical and forward-looking. Share what you're seeing, what you're doing differently right now and what you believe is coming next. It doesn't need to be brand new, just relevant to how we work today.

Open to perspectives across WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix Studio, Framer, custom code and AI-assisted workflows.

Format: 30–40 minutes, pre-recorded. Slides, no slides (just you talking), screen-share demo, tutorial style, or a mix. Up to 3 minutes at the end to invite attendees to a free resource or your list.

  • Test your mic, record a short sample first
  • Keep slides and on-screen text easy to read
  • Close notifications and unrelated tabs
  • Hide client info, passwords, anything private
  • Keep your intro brief, leave room for the useful stuff

Suggested structure:

  • 3 minutes or less — introduction and topic overview
  • Where people go wrong with this specific topic
  • Teaching section
  • A challenge and specific action steps to give a transformation
  • Up to a 3-minute pitch

Include questions for attendees to answer in the summit's live chat, discussions or community conversations throughout to boost engagement.

You're welcome to pitch a free product. For the best results, create a freebie that moves attendees toward the transformation you've promised and ties into your challenge and pitch — something directly related to your topic performs better than an unrelated freebie or paid product. Hook it up to a tripwire so you can start earning from your presentation right away, then add new subscribers to your funnel.

We'll create a workbook with worksheets for every presentation — you're welcome to contribute specific questions and include links and a call-to-action in your worksheet.

Lifetime access to the presentations, companion workbook and additional speaker resources.

Commission: 50% if you contribute a qualifying premium PowerPack resource, 40% otherwise.

Good contributions: templates, toolkits, courses with a specific outcome, actionable workshops, limited memberships. Must be free for PowerPack holders to redeem, not something already freely available elsewhere, and not a discount requiring extra payment.

  • We can't include discounts (e.g. 25% off a course)

Depending on the bonus you provide, you can expect to receive plenty of extra email addresses from those who claim it.

Where possible, coupon codes and offers should stay valid through January 2027 — anything longer than that is entirely up to you.
